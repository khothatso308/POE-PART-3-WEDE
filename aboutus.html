ABOUT US

Cathy’s Cakes is a passionate bakery founded in 2021 by Cathy Motheogane. We create delicious, beautifully designed cakes for birthdays, weddings, celebrations, and special occasions.

Our cakes are made with high-quality ingredients and creative designs to make every celebration memorable.

We believe every cake should be special, from its delicious flavour to its beautiful appearance. Our products are prepared with care and creativity to bring our customers' ideas to life.

Whether you need a birthday cake, wedding cake, cupcakes, or cake pops, Cathy’s Cakes is here to make your celebration sweeter.

Thank you for choosing Cathy’s Cakes. We look forward to being part of your special moments!
