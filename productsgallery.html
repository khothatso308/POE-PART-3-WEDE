CUSTOM ORDERS

At Cathy’s Cakes, we believe every celebration is unique, and your cake should be just as special. You are not limited to the designs displayed on our website.

We can work with your preferred colours, flavours, sizes, shapes, themes, decorations, messages, and overall design.

Custom orders are perfect for birthdays, weddings, baby showers, graduations, anniversaries, corporate events, and themed parties.

Contact us with your ideas, inspiration pictures, preferred collection date, and number of guests. We will discuss your requirements and provide a suitable quote.

If you can imagine it, we can work with you to create it!
